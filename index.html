Frost World began as a single freezer and a hand-cranked churn in Sector 15. We still make ice cream the slow way, in small batches, so every scoop tastes like what is written on the label.

We keep our recipes short: milk, cream, fruit, nuts and sugar. If it does not belong in your kitchen, it does not belong in our tubs.

How we make it

From dairy to cone in four steps.

1

Source

Milk from nearby dairies and fruit from Haryana and Maharashtra orchards arrive each morning.

2

Churn

Small batches are churned slowly so the ice cream stays dense, smooth and low on air.

3

Freeze hard

Every tub is blast-frozen to lock in flavour, then rests overnight.

4

Serve

Scooped fresh at the parlour or packed in insulated boxes for home delivery.
